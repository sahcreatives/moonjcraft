Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this for?

Our wholesale platform is designed for boutiques, concept stores, online home decor shops, and fair-trade retailers looking for authentic, sustainable products.

What are the Minimum Order Quantities (MOQs)?

Our typical minimum is 10 pieces per SKU for first orders. This allows you to test different designs in your store with minimal risk. We can discuss larger volume discounts for bulk orders.

What is the production lead time?

As our products are handcrafted, the approximate production and packing window is 2–4 weeks, depending on the order size and complexity.

How do you handle shipping?

We ship directly from India via reliable international couriers. Buyers are responsible for any import duties and taxes applicable in their country.

Can I customize the products?

Yes, we can work on custom color themes, size ranges, and even specific patterns for larger orders. Please contact us to discuss your requirements.

What are the payment terms?

We require upfront payment via PayPal or bank transfer for wholesale orders. Specific payment details will be provided during the checkout process.
