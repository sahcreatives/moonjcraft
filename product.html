Material & Care

All our products are handwoven from natural, sacred Moonj grass. Small variations in size, color, and weave are expected and celebrated as part of the unique charm of handmade artisanal crafts.

Care Instructions

  • • For dry use only.
  • • Keep away from prolonged moisture.
  • • Wipe with a dry, soft cloth if needed.
  • • To maintain shape, avoid excessive heavy loads.
